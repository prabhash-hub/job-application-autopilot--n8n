    GOOD TO KNOW

    Questions, answered.

    Do I still have to write the cover letter myself?+

    No. That's the point. You submit once and the cover letter is written for that specific role, personalised with your details, and emailed to you. Read it, tweak a line if you like, and send it.

    What does the fit score actually mean?+

    It's a 0–100 comparison of your CV against the job description, and it always arrives with written reasoning: which requirements you cover, where the gaps are, and how closely your experience matches the language of the posting. A score is only useful when you know why.

    What happens if the AI call fails?+

    The autopilot always answers. If the AI can't complete a run, you get a clear message instead of silence, your application still stays on record so nothing is lost, and your follow-up reminder is still created. You can simply submit again.

    Where does my data go?+

    Your details are used only to run your application: the cover letter goes to the email address you provide, your score and verdict are saved so you can track them, and the follow-up reminder lands on your calendar. Nothing is stored by this page.

    Do I need to know how to code?+

    No code required. It's meant to be used, not configured — you fill in the form once and every step after that happens automatically. There's nothing to install and nothing to maintain.

    How long does one run take?+

    Seconds. Your score and letter are produced the moment you submit, and the email, record, and reminder follow straight after — usually before you've switched tabs.
