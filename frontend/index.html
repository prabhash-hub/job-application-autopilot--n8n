    GOOD TO KNOW

    Questions,
    answered.

    Do I still have to write the cover letter myself?+

    No. The cover letter is written for the role, personalised with your details, and emailed to you. Review it, tweak it if you like, and send it.

    What does the fit score mean?+

    It's a 0–100 comparison of your resume against the job description, with written reasoning about relevant requirements and potential gaps.

    What happens if the AI call fails?+

    You get a clear message instead of silence. The workflow is designed to keep the application on record and create the follow-up reminder.

    Where does my data go?+

    Your information is submitted to the configured application workflow to process your application. This page itself does not store your application history.
